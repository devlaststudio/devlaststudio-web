Kayıp Para Radarı product brief

An AI financial agent by DevLastStudio. This page summarises the problem we are solving, the product, how we use Claude, our business model and our 90-day plan.

The problem

People roughly know where their money goes. What they don't see is which part of it is being lost: a subscription still charged months after they stopped using it, a missed return window, a product that got cheaper a week after purchase, or small recurring charges that add up over a month.

The solution

Kayıp Para Radarı analyses the emails, receipts and documents a user chooses to share and finds the money leaks they haven't noticed. It ranks them, explains why each one matters and suggests a next step. Our promise: "Don't show me my spending. Find the money I'm missing."

Explainable AI

First-run experience

  1. The user installs the app and sees a free 90-day financial scan.
  2. They pick a source: email, receipt upload or manual import.
  3. Transactions are extracted and categorised.
  4. Leaks and savings opportunities are scored.
  5. A results screen shows the findings, for example "7 findings, estimated impact".
  6. Each finding opens to show the evidence, the reasoning and a suggested action.
  7. The app offers recurring scans and timely alerts.

How we use Claude

Claude sits at the centre of the product, but it is never used as a calculator. Amounts, thresholds and scores come from deterministic services; Claude handles the parts that need language understanding.

Guardrails

  • The model never makes up amounts; every number comes from the data layer.
  • Uncertain findings carry a confidence label ("likely", "estimated").
  • Clear limits are shown wherever a decision could have financial or legal consequences.
  • No action is taken without explicit user approval.
  • Model outputs are logged for audit.

Stack: Flutter (iOS + Android) · .NET Web API · PostgreSQL · S3-compatible storage · Redis job queue · Claude API · FCM / APNs

Security and privacy

Our most important asset is not the data itself but our users' trust. Security is a design principle from day one.

Encryption

Encrypted in transit and at rest; secrets kept out of source code.

Least privilege

Role-based access and minimum permissions for service accounts.

Data minimisation

Only the context needed is sent to the AI layer.

User control

Clear consent screens, defined retention and one-tap deletion.

RiskMitigation
Wrong findingConfidence score, source shown, user feedback
Wrong AI suggestionDeterministic calculations, AI limits, user approval
Data breachEncryption, access control, logging, minimisation

Integration breaksAdapter architecture, health checks, fallbacks

Business model

Because the value is visible as money saved, a subscription model fits. We show real value before asking users to pay, and we avoid an ad-driven model.

Free

Discover

Limited scans, core findings and a monthly summary.

Premium

Monitor

More sources, continuous scanning, advanced AI, goals and priority alerts.

Family

Share

Multiple profiles, shared goals and a household budget view.

Later revenue options: a capped share of verifiable savings, clearly labelled partner offers that benefit the user, and a B2B2C employee financial-wellbeing benefit.
