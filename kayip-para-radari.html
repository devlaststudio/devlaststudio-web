Kayıp Para Radarı

Don't show me my spending. Find the money I'm missing.

Kayıp Para Radarı is an AI financial agent. It reads the receipts, invoices and order emails you choose to share, then finds forgotten subscriptions, missed return windows, price drops after purchase and small recurring leaks. Every finding comes with the reason and a clear next step.

How it works

No bank passwords. You decide which sources to connect, and you can delete your data at any time.

1

Connect a source

Link an email inbox with read-only permission, or upload receipts and PDF invoices.

2

AI reads and organises

Claude extracts merchant, date, amount and category from messy documents. A deterministic engine checks and calculates every number.

3

Get ranked findings

Findings are scored by financial impact, confidence and urgency, and explained in plain language with a suggested action.

What it finds

The first version focuses on the leaks people most often miss.

MVP

Subscription detective

Spots recurring payments and shows their yearly cost, so forgotten memberships stand out.

MVP

Money-leak detection

Flags duplicated, unusual or no-longer-needed charges, with the evidence behind each one.

MVP

Financial X-ray

Five to eight meaningful insights about your habits instead of dozens of charts.

Next

Refund and price alerts

Reminders before return windows close, and alerts when something you bought gets cheaper.

Built for trust

Financial data is sensitive, so privacy is a design principle, not a feature added later.

The AI never invents numbers

Amounts come from your documents and are calculated by rules. The model explains; it doesn't guess.

Honest confidence

Uncertain findings are labelled "likely" or "estimated", never presented as fact.

You stay in control

Nothing happens on your behalf without your approval. Minimal data, encrypted storage, one-tap deletion.

Kayıp Para Radarı is an information and organisation tool. It is not a bank or a licensed financial advisor and does not provide investment, tax or legal advice. Explanations are generated by AI and clearly labelled as such; every suggested action is shown for your review and is only taken with your explicit approval.
